Version 1.0

Thank you for contributing to Gitolotl. This agreement says what CodeMushroom may do with what you
send. It exists so that Gitolotl can always be distributed, and if CodeMushroom ever needs to, licensed
under other terms, without having to ask every past contributor again.

"You" means the person or organisation sending a contribution. "Contribution" means any code,
documentation, artwork or other material you intentionally submit to the Gitolotl repository, for
example in a pull request.

- You wrote the Contribution yourself, or you have the right to submit it under this agreement.

use it. You are not obliged to support it.

## 6. How you agree

You agree to this agreement by ticking the agreement box in your pull request, or by adding a
`Signed-off-by` line to your commits (`git commit -s`). Either one covers that Contribution and
confirms the statements in section 4.
